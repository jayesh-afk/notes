Data Format Pipeline
Data format conversion happens automatically in hardware at the unpack and pack stages. Zero CPU cycles spent on format conversion.

Semaphore Synchronization Flow
Three hardware semaphores coordinate the three TRISC cores. Cores hardware-stall (no busy-wait) until the semaphore condition is met.
